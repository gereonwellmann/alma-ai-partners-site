Services

Three building blocks for getting started and scaling up

You don't have to tackle everything at once. We start where the benefit for your company is greatest.

AI strategy and potential analysis

Where does AI pay off in your company – and where doesn't it? We create clarity and a prioritised roadmap.

  • Review of processes and data
  • Assessment of opportunities, effort, and risks
  • Concrete, prioritised recommendations

Process automation

Repetitive tasks are automated so that your employees have time for more valuable work.

  • Selection of suitable processes
  • Implementation as a pilot, then scale-up
  • Integration with existing systems

Rollout and training of AI tools

We support the rollout and enable your team to use AI tools safely and sensibly in everyday work.

  • Selection of suitable tools
  • Training for beginners and advanced users
  • Guardrails for responsible use

Approach

Results in four steps

A clear process, so you always know where you stand.

  1. Getting to know each other

    We listen, understand your company, and clarify your goals and constraints.

  2. Analysis

    We identify and assess possible uses and prioritise them together with you.

  3. Implementation

    We first implement the most important initiatives as a pilot and test the benefit in practice.

  4. Rollout

    We support the rollout, train your team, and make sure the solution is used in everyday work.
